## What's New

- **Zero lag while playing:** Uses no graphics card resources and 0% CPU while idle.
- **Switch slides without Alt-Tabbing:** Flip through raid strategies and diagrams mid-pull using global hotkeys (`F13`–`F16` or `Control+Alt+Q`).
- **Custom keybinds:** Automatically creates a `RaidViewer.ini` file on first launch so you can rebind keys to whatever you prefer.
- **Second-monitor ready:** Automatically opens fullscreen on your second monitor.
- **Tiny & portable:** Single `~46 KB` `.exe` with nothing else, just drop your strategy folders into `images/` and run.
